I am an economist working on macroeconomics, monetary economics, structural identification, open-economy macroeconomics, productivity, and political economy.

My current research studies monetary policy transmission, external-instrument identification, DSGE models of sanctions and open economies, productivity measurement, and the economics of conflict and natural resources.

I am based at University of Victoria. You can reach me at vgabriel@uvic.ca.
